Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Make Space ("Make Space," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") is provided by J Y.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Make Space.

1. Information We Collect

Make Space does not require you to create an account, register, or sign in to use core features.

The App allows you to create and store personal content locally, including:

  • Emotional reflections and mood tracking
  • Physical decluttering records (such as items or clothes you let go of)
  • Uploaded photos associated with your items or entries

This personal content is stored locally on your device.

2. Photos and Local Storage

Make Space allows you to upload or capture photos when recording physical decluttering entries.

If you choose to add a photo, the App may request permission to access your photo library or camera. Only the photos you explicitly select are used by the App and stored in local device storage.

Your personal entries and photos are not uploaded to our servers.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

Make Space offers optional premium features and auto-renewing subscriptions managed via in-app purchases.

We use RevenueCat as our subscription management service to process and verify purchases securely through Apple’s App Store.

RevenueCat may collect anonymous identifiers and purchase transaction states to validate your subscription status. Apple handles all payment processing directly; we do not store your credit card or financial information.

4. Third-Party Services

Aside from Apple App Store services and RevenueCat for subscription handling, Make Space does not use third-party tracking or advertising networks.

5. Feedback and Contact

If you contact us via email at innatebeauty25@gmail.com, we will receive your email address and message contents solely to respond to your support requests or feedback.

6. Data Deletion and Your Rights

You can delete your records or reinstall the App to clear local data at any time. For subscription management, you can manage or cancel your subscriptions directly through your Apple ID account settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated Effective Date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
